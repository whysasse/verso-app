# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

Verso is a minimalist article reader that stores everything locally on your device and in your personal iCloud Drive. Your reading data is yours.

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** Verso does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics.

## How Your Data Is Stored

- Articles are saved as Markdown files in a folder you choose on your iCloud Drive.
- Reading preferences (theme, font) are stored locally in your device's UserDefaults.
- No account is required. No server receives your data.

## iCloud Drive

If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, your article files sync across your Apple devices via your personal iCloud account, subject to Apple's iCloud privacy policy. Verso has no access to this sync process and receives no copy of your files.

## Third-Party Services

Verso fetches article content from URLs you provide. This is a direct connection from your device to the publisher's website — no proxy, no intermediary.

## Children's Privacy

Verso does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the update will be noted with a new date at the top of this document and released with the app update.
