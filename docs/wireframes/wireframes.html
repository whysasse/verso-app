3D — Quick Tour
9:41
▶ ☁ 📶 🔋
Quick Tour
Three things you can do with Verso.
1. Save
2. Read
3. Archive
📤
Save
Tap Share in Safari → choose Verso. Article saves to your folder as Markdown.
📖
Read
Tap any article to open. Scroll to the end to mark as Read automatically.
📦
Archive
Swipe left on any article to archive. Keeps it handy but out of your main list.
Start reading
Skip
SkippableUsers can tap "Skip" to go directly to Home. The tour is lightweight — users familiar with apps can bypass it entirely.
Three steps shown togetherSave → Read → Archive all visible at once, not paginated. Matches the compact, scannable style of filter chips on the Home screen.
Step 1: SaveVia iOS Share Sheet. User shares from Safari or any app → Verso parses article → saves as Markdown file to configured iCloud Drive folder.
Step 2: ReadTap article card → NavigationStack push to Reading View. Auto-updates status: Unread → Reading on open, Reading → Read when user scrolls to end.
Step 3: ArchiveSwipe left on article card → reveals Archive action. Moves file to /Archived/ subfolder, updates status to Read.
CTA: "Start reading"Replaces NavigationStack root, NOT a push. Uses navigationDestination path reset to clear the onboarding stack so users cannot swipe back into onboarding. This is critical — do NOT use a standard push transition.
